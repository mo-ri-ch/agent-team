# Build an AI Team

Build a team of AI agents that split the work, talk in one group chat and check each other, like a tiny
company. Interactive notebook-style lessons for beginners and teachers. Standalone. By the end, students run their own team of assistants on their laptop, reachable from their phone.

**Website:** https://gradlabs.in/learn/agent-team/ (coming soon)

## The path

**How a team works** (from scratch, in Python)

1. Why a team?
2. Two bots talk
3. Who speaks next
4. Shared notes and handoffs
5. MCP and A2A

**Your own team** (Hermes Agent, on your laptop, for daily use)

6. Hermes on your laptop
7. Your team of bots
8. A computer for each bot
9. Reach it from your phone
10. Jobs on a schedule
11. Safe by default
12. Run it every day

The notebook look comes from [study-notebook-design](https://github.com/mo-ri-ch/study-notebook-design).
